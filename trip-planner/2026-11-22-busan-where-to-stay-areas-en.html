Where to Stay in Busan: Seomyeon, Haeundae, Gwangalli, Nampo-dong or Busan Station

Booking apps are the best place to check room prices and reviews. This guide only covers which neighborhood to stay in so you travel less and get lost less.

For each neighborhood, we worked out how many minutes the subway takes to the main sights, KTX Busan Station and Gimhae Airport using public data from Busan Transportation Corporation, and put the results in tables.

As of November 22, 2026 — train times and lodging numbers may change.

1. The short answer: which neighborhood fits your trip

Busan's east and west feel quite different as cities. It helps to know this before you pick a neighborhood.

The east (Haeundae, Gwangalli) has many areas along the sea that were developed fairly recently. Centum City in Haeundae was built from 1997 on the site of the old Suyeong Airfield, and Marine City's high-rises went up from the 2000s on reclaimed land. The streets are wide and tidy, and with tall hotels and new buildings it feels like a modern city.

The west (Nampo-dong, Busan Station, Yeongdo) is the old downtown, where Busan's port first grew. Buildings are lower and many are old, and markets, narrow alleys and hillside villages run into each other. More than a set of sights, it is a place to see up close how people in Busan have lived.

Yeongdo is part of the old downtown, but its sea views are superb. It has Huinnyeoul Culture Village, with houses lined up on cliffs above the sea, and Taejongdae, a park on coastal cliffs.

The middle (Seomyeon) sits between east and west and is Busan's main downtown. Department stores, underground shopping arcades and alleys packed with restaurants and bars are close together, and the streets stay busy late into the night.

Right next to it, Jeonpo Cafe Street has small cafes and shops, and Bujeon Market, a large traditional market, is close by. There is no sea or old-alley scenery, but it is a good place to see how young people in Busan eat out and have fun.

East and west are more than 40 minutes apart by subway. So the side you stay on makes a big difference to how long you spend on the subway each day. First count which side most of your days are on, then pick from the table below.

Seomyeon: the middle, wherever you're going

Lines 1 and 2 meet here, so you can reach the most places without changing trains. Busan Station is 10 minutes, Nampo-dong 14, Gwangalli 18 and Haeundae 31.

For Gimhae Airport, take Line 2 to Sasang Station (15 minutes) and change to the light rail. As Busan's biggest downtown, it has plenty of restaurants, shopping and places to go at night.

The downside is that there is no sea. The streets stay busy late into the night, so if you want a quiet stay, pick a place a little away from the main roads.

Seomyeon Station has many exits and a large underground shopping arcade, so it is confusing at first. Find out the exit number closest to your lodging in advance.

Haeundae: a beach break

You can walk to the beach, Dongbaekseom Island and Blueline Park. There are many large hotels, and Gwangalli is 12 minutes away.

Haedong Yonggungsa is about 55 minutes by bus 100 or 181 from outside Haeundae Station Exit 7. You can also go via Osiria Station on the Donghae Line. Directions are in our Songjeong and Haedong Yonggungsa route.

The downside is that the west is far. Nampo-dong, Jagalchi and Gamcheon are 45 to 49 minutes by subway with a change at Seomyeon. Busan Station is also 41 minutes with a transfer.

Gimhae Airport is over 50 minutes by subway and light rail. There is also an airport limousine bus to the Haeundae area. See how to get from Gimhae Airport to Haeundae.

Gwangalli: between the sea and downtown

You can see the Gwangan Bridge night view close to where you stay. On Saturday nights there is also a drone show. Haeundae is 12 minutes and Seomyeon 18, so it is easy to move between the east and downtown.

Drone show times and the best spots are in our Gwangalli night guide.

Get off at Gwangan or Geumnyeonsan Station on Line 2 and the beach is just a short walk away.

The downside is that the old downtown in the west is 32 to 36 minutes away with a change at Seomyeon. When the Saturday drone show ends, crowds pour into the stations all at once.

Nampo-dong: the old downtown on foot

You can walk to Gukje Market, Bupyeong Kkangtong Market and Jagalchi Market. Toseong Station, for Gamcheon Culture Village, is two stops away and Busan Station three.

Huinnyeoul Culture Village is about 12 minutes by bus 7, 71 or 508 from near Nampo Station. Taejongdae in Yeongdo is also close from here.

The downside is that the eastern beaches are far. Haeundae is 45 minutes and Gwangalli 32, with a change at Seomyeon. Gimhae Airport takes two transfers.

A day route is in our Nampo-dong and Gukje Market guide.

Yeongdo: sea views and Airbnb homes

Yeongdo is an island with great sea views. It has no hotels rated 3 stars or higher, but Airbnb homes have grown quickly. Newly registered Airbnb homes doubled, from 18 in January to September 2025 to 39 in the same period of 2026.

There are three reasons for the growth.

Many empty and detached houses. As shipyard jobs disappeared, Yeongdo's population halved, from 210,000 in 1978 to just over 100,000 in 2024. Many of the houses left are detached houses on hillsides, and rents are fairly cheap.

Detached houses make it easy to open an Airbnb. An Airbnb for foreign guests can only be run in a home where the host actually lives, and in an apartment the host needs consent from at least half the neighbors. Detached houses need no such consent, and being up on the hills, many have sea views.

Many more foreigners now visit Yeongdo. According to Busan Tourism Organization, foreign visitors to Huinnyeoul Culture Village in 2024 were 2.6 times the year before. On Korea Tourism Data Lab figures, foreign visits to Bongnae-dong in Yeongdo from January to March 2026 were 12 times the same period a year earlier, the biggest rise of any neighborhood in Korea. It reflects more travelers wanting to stay in a local neighborhood rather than just stop by the sights.

The downside is that there is no subway. You cross the bridge from Nampo-dong and get around by bus. Huinnyeoul Culture Village is about 12 minutes by bus from near Nampo Station (Huinnyeoul Culture Village guide), and to go anywhere else you take a bus to Nampo Station and change to the subway.

It can be inconvenient if you have lots of luggage or plans across several neighborhoods. Pick it when you'll have many quiet days resting by the sea.

Busan Station: KTX and luggage first

This is where the KTX stops, so you don't have to drag your luggage far. On Line 1, Nampo-dong is 3 minutes and Seomyeon 10.

Choryang-dong in front of the station has many restaurants serving pork and rice soup, dwaeji gukbap (돼지국밥), and cold wheat noodles, milmyeon (밀면). For ways to spend spare time on your departure day, see when you have time to kill at Busan Station.

The downside is that the eastern beaches are 41 minutes (Haeundae) and 29 minutes (Gwangalli) away with a change at Seomyeon. The Choryang-dong alleys climbing the hill behind the station are steep, so if your lodging is up the hill from the main road, dragging luggage is hard.

5. Split your stay or stay in one place?

For 3 nights or fewer, one place is easier. Moving takes time to pack and carry your bags, and that day's plans get shorter.

For 4 nights or more with plans split evenly between east and west, splitting your stay is worth trying. See the old downtown from the west (Nampo-dong or Busan Station) and the sea from the east (Haeundae or Gwangalli).

When you split, put your bags in a locker at the station near your next lodging on the morning you move, and you can get around light that day too.

6. What to check before you book

How many minutes' walk from the subway exit
Enter the lodging address in a map app and check the walking time from the nearest subway exit. Even in the same neighborhood, being far from the exit adds to your travel time every day.

Whether it is up a hill
In Busan the mountains come close to the sea, so in many places a steep alley starts just off the main road. Check the street in front of your lodging in advance with the map app's street view.
