How far can your Gimhae layover take you?

Real transit times, a real traffic buffer, and the honest minimum layover you'd need for seven spots around Busan — worked out from live Naver Map data, not a guess.

Last checked September 3, 2026 — fares and schedules can change, so treat older dates with caution.

How we calculated "minimum layover"

We took each route's real-time Naver Map duration and built in a safety margin, the same way you'd want to plan it yourself:

buffered one-way = real-time duration × 1.5
round trip = buffered one-way × 2
minimum layover = round trip + 90 min back at the airport

That number is travel time only — it gets you there and back with a safe cushion before your flight, and nothing more. How long you actually spend at each place is entirely up to you and the spot itself, so budget that time yourself on top of the minimum below.

One more thing: the routes below are Naver Map's "best" pick at the moment they were checked, based on live bus-arrival data — so the ranking between options can shift depending on when you check. Worth a quick recheck on the app before you head out.

The short version

Under 4 hours? Seomyeon, Nampo-dong, or Busan Venice by taxi are your realistic options.

4 to 5 hours? Add Gamcheon, Huinnyeoul, Gwangalli, or Amisan to the list.

Traveling solo and watching the budget? Gwangalli and Seomyeon are cheap and fast by transit — no taxi split needed.
