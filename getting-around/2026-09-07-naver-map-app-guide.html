How to set Naver Map to English

Google Maps is oddly thin in Korea. The real info lives on Naver Map, but it defaults to Korean — flip one setting and you can search regions, find cafes, do all of it in English.

Last checked September 7, 2026 — the app's screens can change with updates.

The Naver Map app icon.

Why bother with Naver Map

Look up a Busan spot on Google Maps and it's often oddly thin — you get a pin, but not the hours, the reviews, sometimes not even a proper name. Naver Map, by contrast, has far more detail almost everywhere in Korea — which is exactly why every location on this site links to both Google Maps and Naver Map.

The catch is that Naver Map defaults to Korean. Switch one setting and you can search inside the app in English — region names, cafes, restaurants, all of it.

What about this site's own "View on Naver Map" links?

As long as the app is installed, tapping one of those links always opens the exact right spot, no matter what language the app itself is set to. The language switch below is for once you're in the app and want to search and browse on your own.

Install it first

Search "Naver Map" (or "네이버지도") in the App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android) and install it. Search and directions work fine without creating an account or logging in.

Switch the language to English

Once it's open, three taps and you're done.

  1. 1

    Tap the profile icon

    In the top right of the map screen, next to the search bar, tap the round person-shaped icon.

  2. 2

    Tap the gear (settings) icon

    When the profile screen opens, tap the gear icon in the top right to get into settings.

  3. 3

    Find Language and switch it

    Scroll down the settings screen to the "App & Display" section — there's a "언어/Language" item. Tap it and switch to English.

Now try searching

Once the language is switched, searching gets a lot easier. Two-step approach:

  1. 4

    Search a region or neighborhood name first

    Type a place name in Roman letters into the search bar. Type just "huinnyeoul", for example, and Huinnyeoul Culture Village comes right up — you don't need the exact spelling.

  2. 5

    Then search a category from that spot

    With the neighborhood you want on screen, search a category like "cafe" and nearby cafes show up with their English names. Same trick works for restaurants, convenience stores, pharmacies — any category.

Based on real screen captures taken September 7, 2026. App updates can move or rename menu items.

Good to know

  • Not every place name comes out in perfect English — some show up Romanized or mixed with Korean. Still a lot more usable than Korean-only.
  • The language setting and search still work fine without logging in.
  • Every "View on Naver Map" link on this site opens the right spot regardless of the app's language, as long as the app is installed.
