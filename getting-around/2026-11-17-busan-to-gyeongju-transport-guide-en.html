Getting Around

Busan to Gyeongju by Train or Bus, Plus Local Buses to Bulguksa and Hwangnidan-gil

Gyeongju is a little over 30 minutes from Busan by train and a little over an hour by bus. But how your Gyeongju trip goes depends a lot on where you get off. This guide covers the easiest way to get there from each part of Busan, and the local buses from where you arrive to the main sights.

As of November 17, 2026 — schedules and routes can change. Check again in a map app before you ride.

Install Naver Map first

Naver Map shows Gyeongju's city bus routes and live arrival times.

How to install it, switch it to English and use directions is covered in How to use the Naver Map app.

Trains stop at Gyeongju Station (KTX), far from the city center. Buses stop at the terminal right next to Hwangnidan-gil.

So even though the train looks faster, the bus is often just as good or easier if you're heading to Hwangnidan-gil or Daereungwon. On the other hand, if you're staying near Busan Station or in Seomyeon, the train is easier.

Travel times are based on timetables. They vary a little with traffic, and because each train stops at different stations.

Gyeongju Station, where the KTX stops, used to be called Singyeongju Station (신경주역). Many foreign blogs still call it Singyeongju Station, but it is the same station.

The old Gyeongju Station in the city center no longer has trains. The name Old Gyeongju Station (구 경주역) survives on a bus stop, but you can't catch a train there.

2. By train

Trains from Busan to Gyeongju leave from two stations. Both arrive at Gyeongju Station (KTX).

Bujeon Station is a different station from Busan Station. When you book, double-check whether your departure station is Busan or Bujeon.

KTX‑Eum and ITX‑Maeum are slightly slower trains than the KTX. You book them on the same Korail website and app as the KTX. Booking and paying with an overseas card are covered in How to book the KTX.

When you get off at Gyeongju Station, you need a bus straight into the city. Which bus to take is in section 4.

If you're staying in Haeundae, the Haeundae Intercity Bus Stop is the easiest. There's no need to go to Busan Station; the bus goes straight to the Gyeongju terminal.

At the end of December 2026, the Haeundae Intercity Bus Stop is set to move one station over, to near Exit 2 of Jung-dong Station. Check the stop's location in a map app before you go.

Buses from Haeundae to Gyeongju leave every hour at 20 past, from 7:20 a.m. to 9:20 p.m. The last bus back from Gyeongju to Haeundae leaves at 9:10 p.m. On a day trip, check the time of the last bus back first.

The easiest way is to buy a ticket at the stop's ticket office. With one bus an hour, you don't need to book ahead; just take the next bus. This route has assigned seats, so your seat number is printed on the ticket.

If you'd like to buy in advance, use the English T-money intercity bus booking site. It takes cards issued abroad (Visa, Mastercard, JCB), but not Korean cards.

When booking, you enter a date of birth and phone number, which you'll use later to look up the booking. After booking, take the card you paid with to the ticket office to collect your ticket. Same-day tickets can only be bought online up to 1 hour before departure.

Sometimes you can ride with a transit card like T-money, but on intercity buses how cards work differs by bus company and route. Don't assume you can just tap a card like on a city bus; buy a ticket and ride.

There is also an intercity bus from Gimhae Airport straight to the Gyeongju Intercity Bus Terminal. It's handy if you want to head to Gyeongju right after landing.

From Gyeongju Station to the city center takes about 20 minutes by city bus. Catch it at the bus stop in front of the station.

Gyeongju Station to Hwangnidan-gil

Buses 700 and 711 pass Hwangnidan-gil and go on to Bulguksa. So rather than heading straight from Gyeongju Station to Bulguksa, it works better to see Hwangnidan-gil and the Bomun area first, then go to Bulguksa.

Taxis leave from the taxi stand in front of the station. It takes about 20 minutes to the city center.

In the city center, walking is fastest. Take the bus to farther places like Bulguksa, Seokguram and the Bomun area.

For the waterwheel and lakeside plaza at Bomun Lake, get off at the Gyeongbuk Culture and Tourism Organization (경상북도문화관광공사) stop.

If you're only going to Bulguksa, take whichever of the two comes first. To stop at Bomun Lake Resort first, bus 10 is faster; to stop at Gyeongju National Museum first, bus 11 is faster.

Since January 2026, buses 10 and 11 start from Hwangnidan-gil Public Parking Lot instead of the terminal. You can still board at the stop in front of the terminal. Older guides say they start at the terminal, which can be confusing.

You can check live bus times in a map app. Tap a stop in Naver Map to see how many minutes until the next bus.

7. Bus 12 to Seokguram

Seokguram sits on Tohamsan Mountain behind Bulguksa. There is no direct bus from the city center, so change to bus 12 at Bulguksa.

Bus 12

Bulguksa TempleSeokguram Parking Lot
See the bus 12 route
  • Where to board: The stop in front of Bulguksa.
  • How often: About one bus an hour.
  • Coming back: Buses from Seokguram to Bulguksa leave on the hour.
  • To Seokguram: Get off at the Seokguram Parking Lot stop and walk about 10 minutes along a forest path.

With one bus an hour, missing it means a long wait. When you reach Bulguksa, check the next bus 12 departure first and plan around it.

9. Check before you go

  • Where you arrive: Trains stop at Gyeongju Station (KTX); buses stop at the terminal in the city. Choose based on where you're staying and where you're going first.
  • Departure station: Busan Station and Bujeon Station are different stations. Check the departure station on your ticket.
  • Last ride back: On a day trip, check the time of the last train or bus back to Busan first.
  • Bus 12: If you plan to visit Seokguram, check bus 12 times at Bulguksa first.
  • Live bus times: For city buses, check in a map app when the next bus comes before heading to the stop.
