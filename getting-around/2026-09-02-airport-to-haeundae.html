Gimhae Airport to Haeundae: How to Get There

Every route blog says "just take the limousine bus." Here's what actually happens when you do — plus two other ways that might serve you better.

Last checked September 2, 2026 — fares and schedules can change, so treat older dates with caution.

The Airport Limousine Bus above, in more detail

Catching Limousine Bus No. 1

  1. From either the domestic or international terminal, head to the 1st floor.
  2. Exit through Gate 4, then turn right — the bus stop is a short walk from there.
  3. Board Limousine Bus No. 1 and get off at the Haeundae Beach (해운대해수욕장앞, Haeundae Haesuyokjang-ap) stop.
Luggage limit: 2 bags per person, 30kg total

Full stop list in English: newbusan.net

Worth knowing: the route was reorganized on January 5, 2026 — a separate service to Jangsan Station split off, an extra bus was added, and a new stop was added at Dongbusan Tourist Complex. If an older guide describes a different stop layout, this is why.

The short version

Traveling light, watching the budget? Light rail + Line 2.

Heavy bags, don't want to deal with transfers? Limousine Bus No. 1.

Two or more people, just want it done? Split a taxi.
