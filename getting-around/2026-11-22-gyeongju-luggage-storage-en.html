Luggage Storage in Gyeongju: Station Lockers, Bus Terminal Storage and Hotel Delivery

Gyeongju is a hard city to get around with luggage. The sights are spread out, and Gyeongju Station, where the KTX (Korea's high-speed train) stops, is about 20 minutes by bus from downtown.

This guide shows where to leave your bags depending on how you leave Gyeongju, the storage places at the station and downtown, and the services that carry your bags to your accommodation.

As of November 22, 2026 — prices and hours can change.

1. The short answer: leave your bags where you will depart

Gyeongju Station (KTX) and the bus terminals downtown are about 20 minutes apart by bus. You have to come back for your bags, so the shortest route is to leave them where your ride out of Gyeongju departs.

2. Gyeongju Station (KTX): lockers and the Zimcarry counter

Lockers

There are lockers in two spots in the 1st-floor waiting hall of Gyeongju Station: near Exit 2 and along the passage to the platforms. A suitcase goes in an extra-large locker.

Prices are for the first 2 hours. After that, a fee is added every hour, up to 12 hours.

Zimcarry Gyeongju Station (짐캐리 경주역점)

If the lockers are full or you have several big suitcases, leave them at the Zimcarry counter at the far left end of Exit 1 on the 1st floor. Staff take your bags and store them.

Staff are there every day from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. After 6 p.m., they move bags into lockers so you can still pick them up until 11 p.m.

3. Downtown: near the bus terminals and Hwangnidan-gil

The intercity bus terminal and the express bus terminal stand side by side in the middle of downtown. Hwangnidan-gil (황리단길), a street of cafés and shops in traditional hanok houses, is about 10 minutes away on foot, so this area works well on days you leave by bus.

Prices and hours are as of November 2026. We checked them against the businesses' price lists, the locker price screens, Gyeongju City's guide and map app listings.

Jimbottari sizes: S is up to 55 cm on the longest side, M up to 65 cm, and L anything larger. SS is for shopping bags and small bags.

The Hwangnidan-gil Community Culture Center is run by Gyeongju City. The self-service lockers on the 1st floor are free, and you don't have to buy anything. Pick an empty locker, enter your phone number, put your bags in and write your name in the logbook.

Be sure to pick up your bags before 6:30 p.m. the same day. You can drop them off until 6 p.m.

Leaving bags for a long time
The bus terminal lockers, Jimbottari and Cafe 242 charge the same however long you leave your bags during opening hours. Jimcare also charges the same within 12 hours. If you drop off in the morning and pick up in the evening, these are cheaper. Jimbottari keeps bags for up to two days for 10,000 won, and after 8 p.m. you can still pick up until 10 p.m. for an extra 2,000 won.

Picking up late at night
If you want to see Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (동궁과 월지) lit up at night before picking up, the 24-hour Ttubeogi Luggage Storage or Hwangnam Market public lockers are the easiest.

Bring 500-won coins for the bus terminal lockers
Cards don't work. The ticket counter will change your money into coins.

Gyeongju National Museum

There are free lockers for visitors at the museum entrance, handy for your bags while you look around. The museum is open from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.

4. Sending your bags to your accommodation

If you want to sightsee without bags as soon as you arrive, or you are moving to another place to stay, a luggage delivery service makes it easy. In Gyeongju, two companies offer it: Zimcarry and Jimbottari.

Zimcarry: between Gyeongju Station and your accommodation

RouteDrop offPick up
Gyeongju Station → accommodationBy 2:50 p.m. at the Gyeongju Station counterFrom 4 p.m. at the front desk
Accommodation → Gyeongju StationBy 11 a.m. at the front deskFrom 3 p.m. at the Gyeongju Station counter

Prices start at 12,000 won for an S suitcase, 16,000 won for M and 20,000 won for L. Storage at the counter on that day is included.

Jimbottari: between the bus terminals, accommodations and Gyeongju Station

RouteDrop offPick up
Storage shop next to the terminal → accommodation or Gyeongju StationBy 3 p.m. at the shopBefore 5 p.m. at the front desk
Accommodation → shop, Gyeongju Station or another accommodationBook by 11 a.m., then leave bags at the front deskFrom 3 p.m. at the shop or Gyeongju Station

Sending to the shop or an accommodation costs 10,000 won for S, 13,000 won for M and 16,000 won for L. Sending to Gyeongju Station or another accommodation costs 12,000, 15,000 or 18,000 won.

Check before you book

Zimcarry only delivers to accommodations registered on its website. Before booking, look for yours in the accommodation list on the Zimcarry website.

Both services hand over bags at the front desk. If you are staying in a small hanok guesthouse without staff on hand all day, ask first whether they can receive your bags.

Zimcarry doesn't deliver plastic bags, paper shopping bags or umbrellas.

Times and prices are from the Zimcarry official website, and from the Jimbottari website and Naver Place price list, which are in Korean only.

5. Seeing Gyeongju on the way from Seoul to Busan in one day

The trickiest day for luggage is when you spend one day in Gyeongju and move on to Busan the same day.

If you go on to Busan by KTX
Leave your bags at Gyeongju Station. See the city, come back to the station, pick up your bags and board the KTX.

If you go on to Busan by bus
From Gyeongju Station, take a city bus or taxi downtown and leave your bags near the bus terminals. At the end of the day, pick them up and get straight on the bus. Buses that go directly to Haeundae are in the Busan to Gyeongju guide.

If you want to send only your bags ahead to Busan
Zimcarry's KTX Express service (KTX특송) puts your bags on a KTX and sends them ahead to its Busan Station counter. Pick them up at Busan Station and head to your accommodation.

Dropping off at Seoul Station: Seoul Station also has a KTX Express counter. Leave your suitcase there in the morning and travel to Gyeongju with nothing to carry, so you won't need to find a place for your bags in Gyeongju.

The Seoul Station counter opens at 8 a.m. The earliest train that can carry suitcases leaves Seoul Station at 9:58 a.m. and reaches Busan Station at 12:46 p.m. Drop off by 9:28 a.m. to make this train. The next one leaves at 10:58 a.m. and arrives at 1:42 p.m.

The Seoul Station KTX Express counter is in a different place from the Zimcarry storage counter on the station's 2nd floor. Check the counter location on the official page before you go.

Dropping off at Gyeongju Station: You can also use KTX Express at the Zimcarry counter in Gyeongju Station. But only one train a day carries suitcases from Gyeongju Station to Busan Station, leaving at 12:12 p.m. You must drop off by 11:42 a.m., so it only works on days you reach Gyeongju in the morning.

Know this before using KTX Express

Bags only go on set trains, and you must drop them off 30 minutes before that train leaves. KTX-Sancheon and KTX-Cheongryong trains only carry small items, not suitcases. The train timetable is on the official KTX Express page. The page is in Korean; choose English from the globe menu at the top for a machine translation.

There is no service on Sundays.

Pick up before the Busan Station counter closes: 8 p.m. on weekdays, 6 p.m. on Saturdays and public holidays.

The price is set by whichever is larger, the longest side or the sum of all three sides. Up to 70 cm on the longest side and 120 cm in total costs 14,000 won, and up to 100 cm and 160 cm in total costs 20,000 won. A carry-on suitcase usually costs 14,000 won, and a checked-size suitcase usually 20,000 won.

Over 10 kg, 2,000 won is added for every 5 kg. Sending from Seoul Station to Busan Station adds 1,000 won for the long distance. For example, a 20 kg large suitcase sent from Seoul Station costs 20,000 won plus 4,000 won for weight and 1,000 won for distance, 25,000 won in total.
