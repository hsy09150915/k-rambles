Left Something on a Busan Subway, Bus or Taxi? How to Get It Back

If you left a bag or phone on a Busan subway, bus or taxi, here is who to contact and where to pick it up, step by step.

As of November 20, 2026. Opening hours and phone numbers may change.

1. When you realize you left something: what to remember first

Busan Transportation Corporation (부산교통공사), which runs the subway, says these three details make lost items easier to find.

  • The train you were on
    If you don't know the train number, say the line number and the direction (the name of the last stop).
  • When you got off
    Tell them which station you got off at and roughly what time.
  • Which car you sat in
    Say which car it was counting from the front, and whether you left the item on the shelf or on the seat.

Over the last three years, the most common lost item on the Busan subway was wallets (23.9%), followed by bags (14.8%), electronics (8.9%), shopping bags (8.7%) and cash (7.6%).

Small electronics such as wireless earbuds, power banks and handheld fans keep going up. Before you get off, take one more look at your seat and the shelf.

2. If you notice right after getting off: go to that station's office

If the train has only just left, go straight to the station office (역무안전실) at the station where you got off and report it.

If it fell into the gap between the train and the platform

Every Busan subway station has screen doors, but phones and earbuds still sometimes fall into the gap between the train and the platform when people get on or off.

Remember the position number written on the screen door where it fell (for example, 3-4) and tell the station office.

For safety reasons, nothing can be picked up from the tracks while trains are running. Staff retrieve it after service ends for the day and return it the next day.

4. How to get to the Seomyeon Station Lost and Found

Everything found on Busan subway Lines 1, 2, 3 and 4 ends up at the Seomyeon Station Lost and Found. It is the only lost and found center Busan Transportation Corporation runs.

It moved to its current spot on August 3, 2026. The center is inside the ticket gates, so if you came to Seomyeon Station by subway, you can walk straight there without going out through the gates.

In the transfer passage between Line 1 and Line 2, head toward the CU convenience store and you'll see the Lost and Found sign hanging from the ceiling.

If you're coming from outside the station, you need to go through the ticket gates. Press the call button next to the gates or go to the station office and say you're there to pick up a lost item.

For a bag or shopping bag, you should be able to describe what's inside. For jewelry, a purchase record or a photo of you wearing it helps. This is what Busan Transportation Corporation advises.

When an item is handed back, staff check that the person collecting it is the owner. This is the procedure set out in the Enforcement Decree of the Lost Articles Act (유실물법 시행령), so be sure to bring ID.

Call before you go

Call first to check that your item has reached the center. It may still be at the station where it was found, or it may have gone to the police after 7 days.

5. Search online on Police Minwon24

Items found on the Busan subway are also listed on Police Minwon24 (경찰민원24), run by the Korean National Police Agency. The old LOST112 site was merged into it on January 26, 2026.

You can search found items without logging in. On a phone, you can also use the Police Minwon24 app.

  • On the first screen, tap Found Item Search (습득물 검색) under Lost Item Services (유실물 민원).
  • Enter the item type (wallet, phone, bag and so on) and the dates from the day you lost it to today.
  • Choose Busan as the region, and for the receipt type choose Agencies other than police (subway, airport, etc.) (경찰이외의기관).
  • If a similar item shows up, check where it is kept and the phone number on the detail screen.

For items with your name on them, such as a passport or ID card, search by your name. For a phone, search by its serial number. This is the fastest way.

It can take time for an item to appear in the system. If it doesn't show up at first, keep searching for a few days.

Report a lost item at a police station

To file a lost item report on Police Minwon24, you need to log in with a simple authentication method such as Korean mobile phone verification.

If you can't verify with a Korean phone, go to a nearby police station, police substation (지구대) or police box (파출소) and report it there.

If you lost it at Busan Station on subway Line 1, contact the Seomyeon Station Lost and Found, not the Busan Station Lost and Found for KTX. It is the same Busan Station, but the subway is run by Busan Transportation Corporation, while KTX and other trains are run by Korail.

A taxi is easier to find if you know its plate number. If you paid by card, the payment record helps track down the taxi. For how to ride a taxi, see the taxi guide, and for buses, the bus guide.

Here too, if no owner comes forward within 7 days, the item goes to the police. From then on, search for it on Police Minwon24 as described above.

7. If Korean is hard for you

Call the 1330 Korea Travel Hotline, run by the Korea Tourism Organization, and they will interpret for you when you talk to the Lost and Found or station staff. They support English, Japanese, Chinese, Russian, Vietnamese, Thai and Indonesian.

Police Minwon24 also points foreigners to this service for interpretation about lost items. From an overseas phone, call +82-2-1330.

1330 answers 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. On weekends or at night, when the Lost and Found is closed, you can call 1330 and ask them to help interpret when you call 1544-5005.

At Busan Station on Line 1, there is an AI help kiosk that answers questions in 13 languages. If the kiosk can't solve your problem, it connects you to station staff by video call, and what they say is interpreted for you. Use it if you have trouble communicating at Busan Station.

In 2024, 10,466 items were found on the Busan subway, an average of 43 a day, and 47.6% of them went back to their owners.

Cash also came in, ₩99.32 million in total, and ₩62.46 million of it was returned to its owners. Lost cash can be recovered too if you report it, so don't give up and get in touch.
