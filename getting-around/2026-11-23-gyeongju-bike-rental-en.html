Bike Rental in Gyeongju: Where Travelers Can Rent, Prices and the Best Routes

Downtown Gyeongju is mostly flat, and its sights sit within 3 km of each other, so it is an easy city to see by bike.

This guide covers where travelers can rent a bike, prices and opening hours, what to bring, and good routes to ride.

As of November 23, 2026 — prices and hours can change.

1. In short: travelers should rent from a bike shop

Downtown Gyeongju is mostly flat, which makes it good for cycling. Daereungwon, Cheomseongdae, Woljeonggyo Bridge and Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond are all within 3 km, a distance that is too far to walk comfortably but too short for a taxi. A bike connects them easily.

Gyeongju also runs a public bike called Tasilla (타실라), but to sign up you need a Korean mobile number registered in your own name and a foreign resident registration number. So for short-term visitors, renting from a shop near the bus terminals or Daereungwon is the easiest option. If you live in Korea, Tasilla in section 5 is much cheaper.

2. Finding a bike shop and prices

Bike shops are clustered in two areas: in front of the express and intercity bus terminals, and around Daereungwon and Hwangnidan-gil.

There are many shops, and prices and closing times differ from shop to shop. Use the buttons below to find a nearby shop on the map and just walk in. Most shops don't need a reservation.

Naver MapGoogle Maps
Places named "... bike rental station" (자전거대여소) are Tasilla

On Naver Map, places named "○○ bike rental station" (○○ 자전거대여소) are not shops but unstaffed docks for the Tasilla public bike.

There are no staff and you can only rent with the app, so visitors will find them hard to use. Choose a shop with staff.

Shops in front of the terminals are generally cheaper, and shops right outside Daereungwon cost a little more. A day rate usually runs until the shop closes.

If you have luggage, leave it at a storage place in front of the terminal before you rent. See our Gyeongju luggage storage guide for places to leave your bags.

Shops around Daereungwon also rent electric carts for 2 to 6 people. They are handy with children or older parents, but like a car they need a driver's license. Visitors should bring an International Driving Permit.

3. What to bring when you rent

Passport
Shops check your ID or take a photo of it before renting. Bring your passport.

A driver's license for scooters and electric carts
A regular bike needs no license. Electric scooters, motor scooters and electric carts can only be rented with a Korean driver's license or an International Driving Permit.

Return time
Closing times vary from 6 p.m. to 10 p.m. depending on the shop. Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond is open until 10 p.m., with last entry at 9:30 p.m. If you want to see the night view before returning the bike, pick a shop that stays open late.

Lock and helmet
At many historic sites you walk inside, so you will need to park your bike at the entrance. Ask for a lock when you rent. You can ask for a helmet too.

In Korea a bicycle counts as a vehicle

Where there is no bike lane, ride on the right edge of the road, not on the sidewalk. Gyeongju City gives the same advice.

Hwangnidan-gil and the area around Cheomseongdae are very crowded with walkers on weekends. At those times it is safer to get off and walk your bike.

These bike routes come from Gyeongju City's magazine. If it's your first time, the downtown heritage route is the easiest.

Routes and distances are from Gyeongju City's magazine, autumn 2026 issue. Times are riding time only, not including sightseeing.

Downtown heritage route
Start at Hwangnidan-gil, stop by Seongdong Market (성동시장), then pass Daereungwon, Cheomseongdae and Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond on the way to the Gyeongju National Museum. Seongdong Market is about 600 m beyond the top of the map.

For what to see at each site, see our Gyeongju downtown walking route guide.

Along the Namcheon Stream route
From Gyochon Village (교촌마을), cross Woljeonggyo Bridge and follow the Namcheon Stream (남천) past the Oreung Royal Tombs (오릉), Najeong (나정) and the Poseokjeong Pavilion Site (포석정지) to Samneung Forest (삼릉숲) at the foot of Namsan Mountain. It is quieter than downtown, with less traffic.

Bukcheon Stream and Hyeongsan River route
Start at Hwangseong Park (황성공원), ride east along the Bukcheon Stream (북천) embankment, cross in front of the Tomb of King Heondeok (헌덕왕릉) and return on the other bank. On the way back you can stop briefly at Bunhwangsa Temple (분황사) and the Hwangnyongsa Temple Site (황룡사지).

Around Bomun Lake
A flat loop around the lake at Bomun Lake Resort (보문관광단지). You can stop at Donggungwon Botanical Garden (동궁원) and Rawon (라원) near the entrance, and the Wooyang Museum of Contemporary Art (우양미술관) by the lake. Bomun is far from downtown, so you need to get there separately.

5. If you live in Korea: the Tasilla public bike

Tasilla is Gyeongju City's public bike, run the same way as Seoul's Ttareungyi: you rent with the app and return it to any station downtown. There are about 155 stations and 600 bikes in the city.

At 1,000 won a day it is very cheap, but only people who meet all the conditions below can use it. If you live in Korea and can read Korean, it is the cheapest way to get around Gyeongju.

Pay in the app or on the website by credit card or mobile phone billing. You can't pay with a transit card. But if you register a card with a transit function once, you can tap that card instead of scanning the QR code to unlock a bike next time.

While your pass is valid, each rental lasts up to 150 minutes. Return the bike within 150 minutes and rent again, and there is no extra charge. After 150 minutes, 500 won is added for every 30 minutes.

How to rent and return

Check nearby stations and the number of bikes left in the app, then go there. Tap Rent, scan the QR code on the bike lock, and press the blue button on the lock when the voice guide tells you to. Keep Bluetooth and location services on.

You must return the bike at a Tasilla station. If you lock it anywhere else, it is only temporarily locked, and your rental time keeps running. You get a notification when the return is complete, so check it.

It runs every day from 6 a.m. to midnight.

Sign-up conditions, prices, payment methods and how to use it are from the Tasilla official website. Station and bike numbers are from a Gyeongbuk Ilbo article from October 2026.
