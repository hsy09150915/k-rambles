Luggage Storage in Busan — Subway Lockers Handle Most of It

87% of stations have extra-large lockers. For anything bigger than that, there’s still an option.

As of September 2026 — Busan Transportation Corporation’s locker data is from the May 31, 2026 release.

This page contains affiliate links. If a booking is made through them, K-Rambles may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Most hotels will hold your bags before check-in or after checkout. But this guide is still useful more often than you’d think.

  • Even if you’re staying at a hotel — when you want to start sightseeing the moment you land or arrive, without lugging bags around. For that, you need a same-day delivery service to your hotel (Section 2 below).
  • You’re doing a day trip to Busan with no accommodation
  • You’re staying at an Airbnb or guesthouse with no front desk to hold luggage
  • Your route doesn’t line up — say your hotel is in Haeundae, but on departure day you want to spend time near Busan Station or the airport. Nobody wants to trek back to Haeundae just to grab their bag.

Here’s where to go in each case.

1. Subway Station Lockers — Better Than You’d Expect

We pulled the actual dataset Busan Transportation Corporation publishes (“Locker Information,” released May 31, 2026, via Korea’s open data portal) and checked it ourselves. 62 of 71 stations (87%) have “extra-large” lockers.

Here’s where they are at the stations tourists actually use.

Price (per that dataset — small/medium/large rates vary slightly by line since each has a different operator, but extra-large is the same everywhere): ₩6,000 per 3 hours

Line 4 has no extra-large lockers at all

All 6 Line 4 stations (Dongnae, Suan, Chungnyeolsa, Yeongsandae, Witbansong, Anpyeong) have none, and neither do Namsanjeong, Sukdeung, or Daejeo on Line 3. If you need to store a large bag near those, you’ll need one of the options below.

This is public data updated annually, so the counts and prices could change over time.

This data is straight from “Busan Transportation Corporation – Locker Information,” dated May 31, 2026 (via Korea’s open data portal, data.go.kr), which we downloaded and checked ourselves. It includes counts by size, prices, and the operator per station — but the exact centimeter dimensions of a locker aren’t in the data. Real user reviews suggest large suitcases do fit, but there’s no official spec to confirm it.

They can fill up during summer peak season

Busan Station’s lockers are often already full during peak times. Give yourself a buffer, or keep the alternatives below in mind.

2. ZimCarry — Land Light

The moment you land at the airport or step off at Busan Station, you don’t have to carry heavy bags around — you can start sightseeing right away. Drop your luggage off and it gets delivered to your hotel the same day, and the delivery fee already includes storage, so there’s no separate storage cost to worry about.

Same goes for your last day — check out, spend the day free of your bags, and just pick them up at the airport or station before you leave.

It’s a proven service on KKday, with over 2,000 bookings and a 4.9 (Excellent) rating.
