20 Dwaeji Gukbap Spots, Picked by Busan Locals

Not tourist reviews — 10,800 Busan residents voted on this. They’re scattered across the city, so just pick whichever fits your route.

As of September 2026 — citizen-recommendation results from the 2026 Busan Dwaeji Gukbap Festival (announced by Visit Busan / Busan Tourism Organization)

Every Busan dwaeji gukbap shop simmers its broth and boils its pork a little differently.

The same dish can taste completely different depending on whose kitchen it comes out of. How long the broth simmers, how the pork is boiled, how the seasoning is balanced — every shop has built its own version over decades.

So when you’re traveling and want to know where’s actually good, the best source isn’t a tourist review or a ranked list — it’s the people who’ve been eating there their whole lives. That’s the information that lowers your odds of a disappointing bowl.

And right now, more travelers are choosing to follow how locals actually live instead of sticking to the standard sightseeing route. Eating where locals genuinely line up is an experience in itself.

How these 20 were chosen

The city of Busan and the Busan Tourism Organization asked directly. From August 16 to September 2, residents were invited to recommend their favorite dwaeji gukbap shop — 10,800 people took part, nominating 254 different places. The 20 with the most overlapping recommendations were published.

These 20 aren’t clustered around tourist areas. From Nam-gu to Dong-gu, Suyeong-gu, and even Gijang-gun, they’re real neighborhood shops spread across the whole city. So instead of asking "which one is best," it’s worth asking "which one is near where I’m already going."

A Few Things Before You Go

  • Many close for a mid-afternoon break. Some shops shut between roughly 2 and 5pm, so check hours on a map app before you go.
  • Parking is limited at many of these. Most are neighborhood shops on residential streets, so public transit or walking is often easier.
  • Shops can move or close at any time. This list reflects the September 2026 announcement — double-check on a map app before visiting.
