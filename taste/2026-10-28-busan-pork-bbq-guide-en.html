Busan Pork BBQ Guide: Samgyeopsal, Moksal, Pork Ribs and Back Ribs

This guide to grilled pork in Busan covers how to grill and wrap samgyeopsal (삼겹살, pork belly), cuts such as moksal (목살, pork neck) and hangjeongsal (항정살), pork ribs and back ribs, from names of cuts to restaurants.

As of October 28, 2026 — Opening days and menus may change. Check before you go.

Photo: Duteopsam, 751-4 Maehak-ri, Jeonggwan-eup, Gijang-gun, Busan (두텁삼, 부산 기장군 정관읍 매학리 751-4)

1. Samgyeopsal (삼겹살) - Pork belly grilled and eaten wrapped in leaves, the classic Korean BBQ

Samgyeopsal is pork belly. The Encyclopedia of Korean Culture (한국민족문화대백과사전) describes it as the cut running from the belly, where the ribs have been removed, back to the hind leg. The name comes from the three layers (gyeop, 겹) of lean meat and fat that make up the cut.

According to the same encyclopedia, restaurants specializing in grilled samgyeopsal appear to have started in the late 1970s.

What does samgyeopsal mean to Koreans and to foreigners?

Koreans love samgyeopsal. According to the Korea Rural Economic Institute (한국농촌경제연구원), each person in Korea ate about 30.0kg of pork in 2024. That is about the same as chicken (15.2kg) and beef (14.9kg) combined.

Among pork cuts, samgyeopsal is the most popular. In a 2024 survey of 507 consumers by the same institute, 60.0% named samgyeopsal as their favorite pork cut. Second place was moksim (목심), also called moksal (목살), at 24.5%.

It also leads in sales. According to the Korea Institute for Animal Products Quality Evaluation (축산물품질평가원), sales of samgyeopsal from Korea in 2025 were about 5.2 million kg, more than the front leg cut (about 2.91 million kg).

It is popular with foreigners too. In a 2026 survey of foreigners by the Handon Self-Help Fund Management Committee (한돈자조금관리위원회), which promotes Handon (한돈, Korean-raised pork), samgyeopsal ranked first as the favorite Korean food (57.2%). It also ranked first among Korean pork dishes (52.6%).

VISITKOREA, the travel guide site of the Korea Tourism Organization, also introduces samgyeopsal as the most popular of Korea's meat dishes. Busan has a great many samgyeopsal restaurants.

Types of samgyeopsal

You will often see ogyeopsal (오겹살) on menus too. Samgyeopsal with the skin still attached is called ogyeopsal. It is the same cut as samgyeopsal, but the skin makes it chewier.

Menus also list saeng-samgyeopsal (생삼겹살), naengdong-samgyeopsal (냉동삼겹살) and daepae-samgyeopsal (대패삼겹살). All of them are samgyeopsal, but they differ in whether the meat was frozen and how thickly it is sliced.

MenuWhat kind of meat is it?
Saeng-samgyeopsal (생삼겹살)Samgyeopsal that has not been frozen. It is usually sliced thick. Most samgyeopsal from Korea is sold as saeng-samgyeopsal
Naengdong-samgyeopsal (냉동삼겹살)Samgyeopsal that was frozen for distribution. Much of it is from other countries, and it tends to cost less than saeng-samgyeopsal
Daepae-samgyeopsal (대패삼겹살)Frozen samgyeopsal sliced very thin by machine. It comes out curled up like shavings from a wood plane (daepae, 대패), which is how it got its name

Daepae-samgyeopsal is thin, so it cooks quickly, and it is fairly cheap, which makes it easy to enjoy casually. In Busan, it seems to have already been popular in the late 1980s in places like the alleys of Choryang (초량).

If you are not sure whether the meat is frozen or fresh, ask when you order.

How to grill

Samgyeopsal is grilled on a hot plate.

Samgyeopsal gives off a lot of fat, so it is grilled on a flat plate rather than a wire grate.

Do not flip it too often. Flip it once, when one side is nicely browned as in the photo above.

Once both sides are cooked, cut it with scissors and grill it just a little more, until the cut sides are cooked through. Grilling it to about the level in the photo above is good.

In Korea, grilled meat is cut with scissors, not a knife. The scissors are made only for food and come with the tongs, so feel free to use them. Many people find it fascinating the first time they see it.

At some restaurants, the staff grill the meat for you. If they do not, you can ask the staff like this.

How do I grill this? eotteoke guwo meongnayo (어떻게 구워 먹나요?)

How to eat

People eat it in different ways. Many eat the meat on its own, and many wrap it in a ssam (쌈, a leaf wrap). Eat it however you like.

Ssam means putting grilled meat, ssamjang (쌈장, a seasoned bean paste) and garlic on a leafy vegetable such as lettuce or perilla leaf, and wrapping it up to eat.

When you eat a ssam, open your mouth wide and put the whole thing in at once. That way the filling does not fall out, and you can taste all the ingredients together.

There are no set rules for what goes in a ssam. Grilled garlic, pachae (파채, seasoned green onion salad), kimchi, bean sprouts, seasoned vegetable side dishes, even salted shrimp (saeujeot, 새우젓), anything on the table tastes good in it. Piling on a bit of everything is the Korean way.

Pachae (파채) is a side dish of thinly sliced green onion tossed in seasoning. It is also called pajeori (파절이), and in Busan it is sometimes called paraejaegi (파래재기). Put it in a ssam or on the meat.

Try grilling kimchi (김치) on the plate next to the meat. It soaks up the meat fat and goes well with the meat. Grilled kimchi tastes quite different from raw kimchi. We recommend trying it.

Photo: Musoe Kimchi Samgyeop, Gwangalli Branch, 202-7 Gwangan-dong, Suyeong-gu, Busan (무쇠김치삼겹 광안점, 부산 수영구 광안동 202-7)

Some restaurants also grill mushrooms and onions with the meat. Eat the grilled vegetables with the meat or put them in a ssam.

Some restaurants also serve minari (미나리, water dropwort) or pa-kimchi (파김치, green onion kimchi). Eat them with the meat or put them in a ssam. Try grilling the minari and pa-kimchi on the plate too.

Ssamjang is not the only dipping sauce either. It differs by restaurant: some give only salt, some give salt mixed with sesame oil, some serve salted seafood (jeotgal, 젓갈), and some give minced garlic mixed with sesame oil and salt.

In the photo, going clockwise from the top left, you see jeotgal, the minced garlic and sesame oil sauce, pachae and ssamjang. Pachae is not a sauce. It is a side dish to eat with a ssam or the meat.

It is fine to make a ssam and enjoy several flavors and textures together. At first, though, try the grilled meat with just a little salt, so you can taste the meat as it is.

Then try the other sauces one by one and compare the flavors. The same meat tastes different depending on the sauce.

After finishing the meat, many people finish the meal with doenjang-jjigae (된장찌개, soybean paste stew), bokkeumbap (볶음밥, fried rice) or naengmyeon (냉면, cold noodles).

2. Moksal (목살), Kkeopdegi (껍데기), Hangjeongsal (항정살) - Pork cuts to enjoy besides samgyeopsal

After samgyeopsal, the menu item you see most often is moksal (목살, pork neck). Next come kkeopdegi (껍데기) and hangjeongsal (항정살), and galmaegisal (갈매기살) is also fairly common.

Side orders: Kkeopdegi and Yukhoe

Kkeopdegi (껍데기) is grilled pork skin. People often order it along with samgyeopsal at pork restaurants, and some places marinate it in seasoning before grilling.

Some meat restaurants also have yukhoe (육회) on the menu, which is not a grilled dish. The Encyclopedia of Korean Culture describes it as thin slices of lean beef, seasoned and served raw. It is often served with Asian pear or an egg yolk.

Yukhoe is a dish Koreans love to eat. Hardly any meat restaurants sell it as their main dish, but travel is a good chance to try an unfamiliar food. It goes well alongside grilled meat.

3. Dwaeji-galbi (돼지갈비) - Marinated ribs grilled over charcoal

Dwaeji-galbi (돼지갈비) is pork rib meat marinated in seasoning and grilled over charcoal or on a grill plate. When marinated in a soy sauce seasoning, it is sweet and savory and goes well with rice.

It is made the same way as Bulgogi (불고기), which is well known among foreigners. In both dishes, the meat is marinated in a soy sauce seasoning and then grilled.

The difference is the cut of meat. Bulgogi mostly uses thinly sliced beef, while dwaeji-galbi uses pork from the rib area, which is why it has the name galbi (갈비, ribs).

MenuWhat kind of meat is it?
Yangnyeom-galbi (양념갈비)Ribs marinated in seasoning. They are sweet and burn easily, so many restaurants have staff grill them for you.
Saeng-galbi (생갈비)Ribs without seasoning. You can taste the meat as it is.

The two names above are divided by whether the meat is seasoned. Sutbul-galbi (숯불갈비) describes how it is cooked. It means ribs grilled over charcoal, and both yangnyeom-galbi and saeng-galbi can be charcoal-grilled.

4. Deung-galbi (등갈비) and Deungppyeo-jjim (등뼈찜) - Pork eaten on the bone

Deung-galbi (등갈비) is the meat attached to the rib bones along a pig's back. In the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety's cut classification, it is classified as a cut on the samgyeopsal side of the pig. It is grilled on the bone, and you pick it up and gnaw it off with your hands.

Many menus call it jjok-galbi (쪽갈비). It is deung-galbi cut into individual ribs and grilled. You can choose between salt-grilled and seasoned versions.

Yangpuni Galbijjim (양푼이갈비찜) is a dish of ribs simmered in a spicy seasoning and served in a large bowl. It is steamed or braised rather than grilled, so staff cut the meat with scissors for you.

Photo: Godam, Dongnae Direct-Run Branch, 558-13 Myeongnyun-dong, Dongnae-gu, Busan (고담 동래직영점, 부산 동래구 명륜동 558-13)

Photo: Deungjjak, 168-107 Bujeon-dong, Busanjin-gu, Busan (등짝, 부산 부산진구 부전동 168-107)

Deung-galbi is hot, so many restaurants give you cotton gloves. You put plastic gloves over the cotton gloves, then hold the ribs and eat.

Gamjatang (감자탕) is a soup made by boiling bones for a long time, so it is left out of this article. Below are deung-galbi and braised-rib restaurants with many reviews. Sinchon Yangpuni (신촌양푼이) sells only braised dishes, with no grilled menu.
