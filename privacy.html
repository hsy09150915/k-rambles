Privacy

K-Rambles doesn't run ads, doesn't require an account, and doesn't collect anything like your name or email just for browsing the site.

Analytics. We use GoatCounter, a lightweight, privacy-friendly analytics tool, to see roughly how many people visit and which pages are popular. It doesn't use cookies to track you across visits or across other sites.

Likes and the cost-splitting tool. When you tap the heart on an article or use the expense-splitting calculator, that data is saved with your browser's local storage — on your device only. It's never sent to us, and it disappears if you clear your browser's data or switch browsers or devices.

Affiliate links. Some articles link out to booking platforms like KKday. If you book through one of those links, K-Rambles earns a small commission — this doesn't change what you pay. Once you click through, you're on that platform's own site, governed by their own privacy policy, not ours.

Maps. Some pages embed Naver Maps to show locations. Loading a map may involve a request to Naver's servers, subject to Naver's own privacy policy.

Questions about any of this? Contact us.
